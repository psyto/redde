# A collapse leaves a record

A collapse is remembered as an event: a pool fell, a market became insolvent, a
peg broke. The chain records a different kind of evidence: transfers across
accounts. Those records can be recomputed, but they do not read a person's mind.

**Praeda publishes the boundary-flow record.**

## Three claims

**1. Boundary flow is measurable; motive is not.**
For a declared system boundary and a declared window, the record can show what
crossed the boundary, in which direction, and when each account's net flow became
committed. These are reproducible measurements. They do not establish knowledge,
coordination, beneficial ownership, or guilt.

**2. The engine renders structure, never intent.**
Praeda computes net boundary outflow `E` and timing `L`. It labels only measured
positions in a declared sort: `EARLY_TOP_OUTFLOW`, `NET_OUTFLOW`, and `NET_INFLOW`.
Those labels describe arithmetic and timing, not people or motives. A program,
router, relayer, or custody account is not converted into a person; an ambiguous
route remains explicitly unresolved.

**3. Missing evidence is a refusal, not a story.**
When history, price references, state witnesses, or a deterministic route are
missing, Praeda returns `UNRECONSTRUCTED`, `UNPRICED`, or `ROUTE_UNRESOLVED` as
appropriate. These labels say what the reconstruction cannot establish. They do
not imply that someone hid anything or that an unobserved account is responsible.

## What Praeda will not do

It will not assign intent, name a mind, infer common control, fabricate a route,
or attach a price reference that is entangled with the collapse itself. A court's
findings may supply context for a case file, but the engine's output remains its
own smaller claim: a reproducible transfer calculation.

## Why draw this at all

Public incidents are often told as mechanisms: an oracle moved, a pool emptied,
a market failed. A declared boundary-flow ledger adds an auditable record of what
the chain actually served. The point is not to replace adjudication or narrative.
It is to make the measurable part available for anyone to walk again, leg by leg.
