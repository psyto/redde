What this is

Praeda reconstructs the boundary-flow record of an on-chain collapse — what crossed a system's boundary, in which direction, and when — from public state alone, with no one's cooperation. It publishes two measures per account, net flow and timing, and asserts nothing else: no beneficiary, no intent, no name. Where a flow can't be resolved or a price pinned, it says so.
