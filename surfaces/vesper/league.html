The invariant

Closed-Market Liquidation Soundness

For a venue lending against a tokenized equity E, during the closed-market window the price it uses to trigger and settle a liquidation must be bounded to the last regular-session close — or liquidations must be suspended. Otherwise the venue is gap-exposed: it can seize collateral at a price the regulated market never printed, with no weekend hedge to unwind against.

ET week Fri 20:00 → Sun 20:00 = no live underlying

The largest gap between writes all window was 20.8 minutes — nowhere near the two-hour tolerance in MAX_AGE_LIQUIDATE. Jupiter's own documentation names the exposure and ships no mitigation: “the oracle price may freeze while the xStock token remains tradeable on-chain… a deviation between the market price and the oracle price, especially during periods of stress or news events.”

What now

A RED verdict is a decision, not an alarm

The gap has a proven fix and a safe alternative — both already live on the same chain. So each actor has a move today.

If you borrow on a RED venueDe-risk before the close

Your weekend liquidation price is a 24/7 mark with no tie to Friday's close.

Cut leverage or exit leveraged xStock positions before Fri 20:00 ET — don't ride one through the closed-market window.

If you issue the token · xStocks · OndoSteer where it's collateral

Your token's safety is set by the venue, not by you — and only you can bound it.

Publish a recommended-collateral allowlist, make a closed-market band a listing condition, and withhold endorsement from venues that liquidate through the window.

If you run the venue · Jupiter · FluidAdopt the close-band

The fix is already shipped and audited one protocol over.

Clamp the closed-market price to the last regular close within a band, or suspend liquidations during the window. Vesper re-verifies on deploy.

Path to GREEN

Jupiter Lend needs one change: a market-status band on its xStock oracle, bounding the closed-market price to the last regular-session close — exactly what Kamino runs today (double-audited, GREEN).

Estimated verdict on ship: 🟢 GREEN · re-checked automatically from chain state.

Why re-execution

“Which oracle?” cannot answer this

vendor ≠ safety

Chainlink's own tokenized-equity feed reports the last close with no band and doesn't flag holidays — “the consuming protocol must detect closed markets itself.” Pyth freezes at last price. Safety lives in the protocol's code, not the feed brand.

same inputs, opposite outcomes

Kamino builds a market-status band on top and scores GREEN. Jupiter reads a premium feed with no band and scores RED. A checklist of “uses Pyth / Chainlink” would rate them identically. Re-executing the liquidation path is the only thing that separates them.

Scope & intent

This is neutral soundness measurement, not a vulnerability disclosure. Every figure is derived from public on-chain state and open-source code. The closed-market gap is a structural design property that the affected venue documents itself; Vesper quantifies and confirms it, and grades all venues on one published rule.

Intended reliers are the issuers whose tokens are used as collateral (xStocks / Backed, Ondo) and the venues themselves — parties with standing to bound where their assets are lent. Findings are shareable with them ahead of any public posting.
